# Stand-in photography for catalogue entries

From Wikimedia Commons, re-encoded to WebP at 1000px with metadata stripped —
the same treatment a submitted photo gets. Each is a photograph **of the place
it is filed under**; nothing was padded out with a picture of somewhere else
that looks similar.

Used only where an entry has no photograph of its own, and displayed with its
author and licence because that is what the licences require. Delete the folder
and the wiring in `lib/places/stand-in-photos.ts` once the catalogue has its own
photography.

Most of these went on 2026-09-02, when the seeded placeholder entries they
belonged to were deleted from the database. What is left:
