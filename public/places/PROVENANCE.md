# Stand-in photography for catalogue entries

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-09-01, re-encoded to WebP at 1000px
with all metadata stripped — the same treatment a submitted photo gets.

Every one of these is a photograph **of the place it is filed under**. Where
Commons had only one usable image, that place has one; nothing was padded out
with a picture of somewhere else that looks similar.

These are placeholders. They are used only where a place has no photograph of
its own, and each is displayed with its author and licence because that is what
the licences require. Delete the folder and the wiring in
`lib/places/stand-in-photos.ts` once the catalogue has its own photography.
