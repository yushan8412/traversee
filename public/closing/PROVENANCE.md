# Closing band photograph

`nanya.webp` — 南雅奇岩 Nanya rock formations, Ruifang, New Taipei, by
**fullfen666**, **CC BY-SA 2.0**.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E6%96%B0%E5%8C%97-%E5%8D%97%E9%9B%85%E5%A5%87%E5%B2%A9_%2830118500183%29.jpg

From Wikimedia Commons, re-encoded to WebP at 1800px with metadata stripped.
The credit is printed in the corner of the band itself, because CC BY-SA requires
attribution wherever the work is shown — not only in this file.

Chosen for its colour as much as its subject: blue sea and ochre rock, against a
featured band directly above that is a field of green. A green photograph here
made the two sections read as one. A real photograph of the north-east coast
rather than generic outdoor stock, for the same reason as the catalogue
stand-ins — and, like them, still borrowed and still waiting to be replaced.
